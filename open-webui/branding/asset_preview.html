Source

branding/io.png
2400 × 2729 RGBA · white glyph only
Asset strategy. The source glyph is pure white on transparent, so it is invisible on light backgrounds. Light contexts (splash.png) get a #171717 glyph; dark contexts (splash-dark.png, favicon-dark.png) keep the white glyph; context-independent icons (favicon / apple-touch / PWA) get an opaque dark tile with the white glyph so they remain visible on any background or OS tile colour.
